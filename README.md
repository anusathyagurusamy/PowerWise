⚡ PowerWise

Smart Electricity Monitoring System

PowerWise is a web-based dashboard that helps you track your home electricity usage, manage your monthly budget, estimate bills, and get smart energy-saving suggestions.

🔗 Live Demo: anusathyagurusamy.github.io/PowerWise

✨ Features
📊 Dashboard — At-a-glance view of today's units, monthly units, estimated bill, and remaining budget, with a monthly budget usage tracker.
🏠 Appliance Management — Add appliances (LED Bulb, Fan, AC, TV, Refrigerator, Washing Machine, Laptop, Heater, etc.) along with power rating, quantity, and daily usage hours. Daily units and monthly consumption are calculated automatically.
📈 Reports — Detailed electricity report showing monthly units, estimated bill, budget vs. remaining balance, budget usage percentage, and your highest-consuming appliance.
💡 Smart Suggestions — Personalized, appliance-based tips to reduce electricity consumption.
🌱 Electricity Saving Tips — General tips such as using LED bulbs, optimizing AC usage, avoiding standby power, and using natural light.
👤 Profile — Manage your personal/account details.
